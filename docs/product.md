# Product

## Summary

Mirski Japanese is a Japanese-language education brand. This project is its public
landing page: a fast, accessible, SEO-friendly introduction to the brand, its learning
system, and its four-level course path.

The site's primary job is to help a prospective learner understand the path and take a
confident next step toward an enrolment enquiry.

## Known facts

Only the following business facts have been supplied:

- The brand is **Mirski Japanese**.
- It is a Japanese-language education brand.
- The published course path is:
  **Starter N5 → Elementary N4 → Intermediate N3 → Advanced N2**.

Everything else — contact details, pricing, schedules, class format, instructor
credentials, locations, student numbers, results, testimonials, partnerships, and legal
details — is unverified. It must not be invented.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- People who want to learn Japanese.
- Learners preparing for structured Japanese proficiency goals.
- Prospective students comparing Japanese course options.

No demographic assumptions (age, location, occupation, budget, or language background)
should be added without evidence.

## Goals

1. Communicate what Mirski Japanese is and who it is for.
2. Make the four-level path immediately understandable.
3. Explain the learning approach in credible, non-hyped language.
4. Build trust through clarity, restraint, and honest limits.
5. Guide visitors toward an enrolment enquiry.
6. Keep the experience fast, accessible, and pleasant on mobile.

## Conversion model

Primary conversion: an enrolment enquiry.

The official enquiry channel is **not yet available**. The page therefore:

- Uses internal navigation as the functional primary action.
- Reserves a clearly labelled enrolment block at the end of the page.
- Shows an honest “contact details are not published yet” message until the real
  channel is supplied.

Secondary conversion: understanding the course path and choosing a probable starting
level.

Do not add a fake contact address, phone number, WhatsApp link, booking URL, or form
endpoint.

## Information architecture

| Section    | Purpose                                                        |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Header     | Brand identity, section navigation, enquiry shortcut           |
| Hero       | Position the brand around the N5→N2 path; offer two next steps |
| Why a path | Name the learner problem: too much information, no sequence    |
| Method     | Explain the teaching rhythm in three principles                |
| Courses    | Present the four levels with focus and progression             |
| Outcomes   | Describe capabilities built over time, without guarantees      |
| FAQ        | Answer level and progression questions with native disclosure  |
| Final CTA  | Restate the path and provide the enrolment placeholder         |
| Footer     | Repeat navigation, course links, and contact status            |

Social proof is intentionally absent. It should be added only when real, attributable
testimonials, reviews, or statistics are supplied and approved.

## Non-goals for the initial release

- No CMS, database, authentication, payments, or learner portal.
- No enquiry form until a secure server-side destination exists.
- No third-party analytics, chat widgets, or marketing pixels.
- No blog, course detail pages, or additional routes until content exists.
- No fabricated social proof, ratings, structured reviews, or outcome claims.
- No pricing or schedule information.
